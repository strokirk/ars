# Covenant of the Hollow Crown
### Silberbachtal, the Upper Rhine Marches — Anno Domini 1221

---

## The Place

The **Silberbachtal** — Silver Brook Valley — is a three-league notch cut into the lower slopes of
the Black Forest, two days' ride east of Freiburg. The brook itself runs fast and cold off the high
ridges, dropping through a series of small cataracts before pooling in what was, until very recently,
a wide bog at the valley's throat. The surrounding countryside: mixed conifers and beech, a handful
of hamlets that owe their existence to the covenant's largesse, and one suspicious Benedictine priory
perched on the ridge to the south whose prior watches the valley's unnatural abundance with narrowing
eyes.

The covenant sits on a low bluff above the drained marsh. The structure is a converted Roman *mansio*
— a traveler's posting-house — expanded with a new stone hall, a squat tower the grogs call the
*Rabennest* (Raven's Nest), and a sprawl of workshops, granaries, and stables that, together, look
more like a prosperous minor lord's seat than a wizard's sanctum. This is entirely deliberate.

**Mood:** Quietly triumphant and quietly anxious in equal measure. The valley has been conspicuously
blessed for three seasons running; the granaries are embarrassingly full; the silver strike has drawn
merchants from Freiburg. The magi's schemes are working — which is exactly why the grogs sleep
badly. Something in the drained marsh is wrong, someone at the Benedictine priory is writing letters,
and the Quaesitor who passed through last autumn asked two too many questions before riding on.

## The Three Magi

### Corvus of Tytalus
**Sigil:** When Corvus casts, the air carries a brief smell of turned earth and cold iron — like a
mine shaft at midnight.

**Quirk:** He keeps a wax tablet on his person at all times, scrawling notes mid-conversation. He
will stop a grog in the middle of a crisis report to correct the man's grammar.

**Current Scheme:** Corvus has identified a secondary silver lode beneath the valley's eastern
ridge through *Probe for Pure Silver* and is maneuvering to obtain prospecting rights through
**Reeve Eckard of Waldkirch** — the local crown's land-agent — whom he has already made pliable with
*Aura of Rightful Authority*. His problem: a brotherhood of Freiburg silver-merchants sent their own
assayer to the valley last month. The assayer has not yet filed a claim, but he is still in the
valley, asking questions.

**Off-stage role in the scenario:** Corvus dispatched the grogs on this errand personally. He is the
one who knows the situation is dangerous and said nothing useful.

---

### Konstantinos of Bonisagus
**Sigil:** Spells cast by Konstantinos leave a faint luminescent residue visible to the magical
senses for several minutes — a side-effect of his obsessive precision. Grogs swear that sometimes
they can see faint glowing marks on the walls of his sanctum at night.

**Quirk:** He speaks to people as if dictating a text. Pause. Full sentence. Pause. He does this
even in emergencies. The grogs find it unnerving; he seems to find their hurry inexplicable.

**Current Scheme:** Konstantinos has seeded the valley with three **listening anchors** —
inconspicuous carved stones placed at the village well, the mill, and the road junction — each
connected by *The Ear for Distant Voices* to his scrying station in the tower. He knows everything
said aloud in the valley's public spaces. His current research question: he believes the drained
marsh sits atop an old **regio boundary**, and he wants the vis it may contain before the marsh
fully settles. He is the one who first realized something woke up down there.

**Off-stage role in the scenario:** Konstantinos knows the grogs are walking into a supernatural
hazard. He chose not to tell Corvus because he wants to observe the reaction undisturbed. He will
be watching through *The Ear for Distant Voices* the whole session.

---

### Aldric of Flambeau
**Sigil:** Living plants bend subtly toward Aldric when he concentrates — the way sunflowers track
the sun, but faster, and wrong.

**Quirk:** Aldric's **Blatant Gift** means every mundane in the valley flinches when he appears.
He has long since stopped registering this. He addresses grogs with the direct, slightly too-loud
voice of a man who has learned that people will not look him in the eye and has decided not to wait
for eye contact. He does not mean to be cruel about it. He is simply efficient.

**Current Scheme:** The drained marsh is now twenty-two acres of rich black loam. Aldric has used
*Twist the Living Tree* and *Conjure the Sturdy Vine* to force-grow a hedgerow boundary and
*Bridge of Wood* to give it a proper crossing, claiming the reclaimed land as a covenant demesne
before any mundane lord thinks to ask about it. He is one season away from having a thriving hay
meadow on what was wasteland. He is very pleased with himself.

**Off-stage role in the scenario:** Aldric drained the marsh. The thing that woke up is his fault.
He does not know this yet.

## Seasons of Deeds Already Done

These are the covenant's accomplishments in its first two years — the acts that blessed the valley
and seeded this session's trouble.

**Spring, Year 1 — "The Draining"**
Aldric used *Parting the Waves* and *Push of the Gentle Wave* across three casting sessions to
redirect the marsh's feeder streams, channeling the accumulated water down the valley's south
drainage toward the brook below. The marsh emptied in six weeks — unnaturally fast; the locals
attributed it to an exceptionally dry spring. Aldric immediately used *Conjure the Sturdy Vine*
to stabilize the new bank and *Bridge of Wood* to cross it. The covenant now controls 22 acres of
the best topsoil in the valley. The locals are grateful and quietly terrified.

**Summer, Year 1 — "The Silver Probe"**
Corvus walked the eastern ridge with *Probe for Pure Silver* and identified a promising strike.
He spent the season courting Reeve Eckard of Waldkirch with *Aura of Rightful Authority* applied
twice — once at the market in Waldkirch, once when Eckard came to inspect the covenant's new
"mill-works." Eckard now considers the covenant's requests entirely reasonable and its boundaries
entirely legitimate. He has not examined why he feels this way.

**Autumn, Year 1 — "The Blessed Harvest"**
The first harvest on the reclaimed land yielded three times what any local expected. Aldric's
lingering Herbam effects on the soil had given the first planting an extraordinary yield. The
covenant's mills processed the surplus; the grogs ate well; the magi distributed enough grain
to the hamlets to ensure that nobody starved or, more importantly, nobody left for Freiburg with
too many questions. The Benedictine prior wrote to his abbot: *"The new settlers at the old
mansio enjoy God's favor in unusual measure."*

**Winter, Year 1 — "The Listening Web"**
Konstantinos placed the three carved listening-stones. He also completed *Sense of the Lingering
Magic* passes over the entire valley and identified the drained marsh as the site of an old
magical boundary — almost certainly a **regio threshold**. His notes indicate that the boundary
was "quiescent, possibly dormant." He wrote "MONITOR — do not disturb" and did not tell the
other magi.

**Spring, Year 2 — "The Assayer"**
A Freiburg silver-merchant guild sent one **Brother Albrecht**, a lay-brother with an eye for
ore, to survey the eastern ridge after rumors of the covenant's "lucky finds" reached the city.
Corvus became aware of Albrecht through Konstantinos's listening web. He has been maneuvering
to discredit Albrecht's assay before it can be filed — and in the meantime, the silver-merchants
have sent three armed men to keep Albrecht company and ensure the survey completes.

## The Deed That Seeds This Session

**The Draining woke something.**

Konstantinos's winter survey noted a dormant regio threshold under the marsh. When Aldric
emptied the marsh, the threshold lost the water that had been suppressing it — a natural "seal"
the regio's former inhabitants may have deliberately maintained. Three weeks ago, the grogs
reported that **the old willow at the marsh's south edge was back** — they pulled it out as
part of the hedgerow clearance in spring, but it has regrown, overnight, to full height, with
roots that extend unnaturally deep into the new meadow.

The magi know something is active under the reclaimed land. Konstantinos, characteristically,
wants to study it. Aldric, characteristically, considers this a minor setback in his twenty-two
acres. Corvus, characteristically, wants it dealt with quietly before the Quaesitor comes back.

Their solution: send the grogs to retrieve a **sealed vis-flask** that Konstantinos buried at
the willow's roots last autumn — before the willow came back and covered it. He needs it for
his measurements. The errand should take an afternoon.

It will not take an afternoon.
