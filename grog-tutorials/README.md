# Grog Tutorials — Two Remixes of *The Errand*

Two first-session, **all-grog** Ars Magica tutorials, both remixed from the same root:
[`../the-errand-gm-tutorial.md`](../the-errand-gm-tutorial.md). Each teaches the dice in the
same gentle order (character sheet → Simple Die → Stress Die & botch → combat → "the world is
bigger than you"), and in each the **magi stay off-stage** — it's their *deeds* that drive the
scenario, not their persons.

Both take the three ambitious spring magi from [`../characters/`](../characters/) — **Corvus of
Tytalus** (earth-shaper / prospector), **Konstantinos of Bonisagus** (vis-economy / scrying), and
**Aldric of Flambeau** (water-and-harvest reclaimer, Blatant Gift) — and read them as a single small
covenant pursuing the "econ-history-nerd Pinky-and-the-Brain" power fantasy of
[`../character-concept.md`](../character-concept.md): dominate a 13th-century valley through real
medieval economic levers (drained marshes, silver, mills, a bent reeve, a conspicuously blessed
harvest), in tension with the Order's Code.

The two are **independent covenant interpretations** — different valleys, names, and details — joined
only by that shared origin. The difference is the camera angle.

## The two remixes

### 🏰 [`allies/`](allies/) — grogs *inside* the covenant
**The Willow That Came Back**, at the **Hollow Crown** covenant in Silberbachtal (Black Forest, 1221).
The players are the covenant's own soldiers and servants. A routine errand to retrieve a buried
vis-flask goes wrong **because of the magi's own meddling** — Aldric's drained marsh woke something
under a willow that regrew overnight, and a fraudulent guild assayer and roadside claim-jumpers are
drawn by the valley's suspicious wealth. POV: loyalty to and fear of your own masters; *this is the
wizards' department, not yours.*

### ⚔️ [`opponents/`](opponents/) — grogs *against* the covenant
**The Steward's Errand**, versus the **Silbersturz** covenant of the Wachteltal (east of the Rhine,
near Mainz). The players are an *outsider's* company — Lord Baldewin's steward sends five men to
investigate a valley that has grown impossibly rich. They probe Rohrbeck and the too-perfect mill,
discover someone knew their names before they arrived, and at midnight run headlong into the
covenant's **magic itself** — a moving hillside, a wrong-flowing river, the dread of the Blatant Gift —
which turns them back. POV: you are the mundane threat the magi's Code-tension predicted.

## What's in each folder

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `covenant.md` | The invented covenant: name, valley, mood; the three magi fleshed out (sigil, quirk, scheme, off-stage role); the seasons of deeds; the deed that seeds the one-shot. |
| `scenario.md` | The remixed one-shot: index-card prep + scene-by-scene play, each scene teaching one rule cluster, plus a "grow it into a saga" hook. |
| `cast.md` | Run-ready stat cards (Init/Attack/Defense/Damage/Soak, weapon mods pre-bundled) — playable as-is. |
| `gm-notes.md` | First-time-storyguide guide: what each scene teaches, pacing, handling failure and grog death, keeping the magi felt but off-stage. |

> **The shared pedagogy, one line:** start small, mortal, and disposable — the magi are a weather
> system you survive, not a boss you fight. Earn the magic.
